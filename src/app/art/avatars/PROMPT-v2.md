# EXM portraits v2

18 November 2026. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. The owner requested replacing the noisy quick-transfer avatar styling with an approach closer to the supplied Ui Mahadi reference. Random assignment remains the owner's explicit preference.

Source style reference: `ref/ui-mahadi/crypto-wallet-send-flow.png`, viewed before generation. The first atlas remains archived as `exm-portraits.png`.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized illustration asset atlas for EXM self-custody wallet, to replace overly detailed close-up portrait avatars. Reference image: the attached wallet UI is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Study the four SMALL QUICK TRANSFER characters, not the coin logos or interface. Generate an ORIGINAL, beautifully art-directed square sprite atlas of 16 different human characters, exact 4 columns x 4 rows, equal square cells, no gaps or margins. Each cell has its own solid flat background color and one centered head-and-UPPER-TORSO editorial cartoon; the head is SMALL, about 35% of cell width, and shoulders/chest visible filling the lower half, like the tiny avatars in the reference. Character stays inside its cell. The app circular-crops each whole square. STYLE: refined flat vector-like 2D editorial drawing, thin charcoal hand-drawn contour, clean flat shapes, very few facial marks, playful silhouettes, natural asymmetric poses, elegant and legible at 46 px. No realistic rendering, NO paper/noise/halftone texture, no shading, no large faces, no glossy 3D, no intricate hair strands, no tattoos or dense details. Distinct people: adults, a variety of genders/skin tones/hair, some glasses, a few stylized pink/coral/lavender skin colors. Simple expressive clothes in cobalt, violet, mint, white and coral; backgrounds vary among cyan, lavender, butter yellow, coral, mint and periwinkle, with cohesive saturation. A few characters looking left/right or touching a cheek, mostly relaxed. Original own characters, not copies. Exact atlas layout is essential: each of the 16 cells is completely filled edge to edge by a SINGLE solid color background, person cleanly centered with consistent optical scale. No text, no border, no grid lines, no UI, no logo, no coins, no reference phone. Output the final asset atlas only.

Output: `exm-portraits-v2.png`, native 1254 × 1254 pixels. Alpha is not required because each cell is intentionally circular-cropped over its own color field. The original is retained in the generated_images directory; the application imports its workspace copy.
